✦ Help & Clarity ✦

Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions before visiting? Here are the answers to our most common dining queries.

❓ Do I need a table reservation in advance? +
Walk-ins are always warmly welcomed! However, for Friday to Sunday dinner hours and festival holidays, we strongly recommend reserving a table in advance using our online form or calling us to avoid wait times.
❓ How many guests can I book a table for? +
You can reserve tables for 1 guest up to 20 guests directly online. For large group gatherings or private dining celebrations, please contact our restaurant manager via WhatsApp or Call.
❓ Is parking facility available at the restaurant? +
Yes! We offer complimentary 100% free valet parking service for all our dining guests right at the restaurant main entrance, along with a spacious reserved parking lot.
❓ Do you offer Private Dining Rooms (PDR)? +
Yes, we have 2 sound-proof, air-conditioned Private Dining Rooms (PDR) suitable for 8 to 16 guests, perfect for confidential business meetings, anniversary dates, and family celebrations.
❓ Can I bring a birthday or anniversary cake from outside? +
Yes! You are most welcome to bring your celebration cake. Our team will gladly store it in cold refrigeration, arrange standard sparkler candles, and assist with cake service with no extra charge.
❓ Is outdoor or rooftop garden seating available? +
Yes! We feature a lush green Outdoor Garden Patio on the ground level as well as a romantic Rooftop Terrace dining area under starry skies (weather permitting).
❓ What is the table booking cancellation policy? +
We understand plans change! Table reservations can be rescheduled or cancelled free of charge up to 1 hour prior to your reserved time slot by calling us or replying to your WhatsApp confirmation.
